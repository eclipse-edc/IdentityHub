KeyRequiredTypeDefaultPatternMinMaxDescription
edc.issuer.issuance.state-machine.iteration-wait-millisxstring1000The iteration wait time in milliseconds in the state machine.
edc.issuer.issuance.state-machine.batch-sizexstring10The number of entities to be processed on every iteration. They are leased together, so the lease must outlast the processing of all of them.
edc.issuer.issuance.state-machine.lease-duration-millisxstring300000How long the entities of a batch are leased, in milliseconds. It must be longer than the processing of a whole batch, i.e. longer than the batch size times the call timeout, otherwise another runtime may process the same entities.
edc.issuer.issuance.send.retry.limitxstring80How many times a failed operation is retried before it fails with an error. With the default delays, the retries take about six hours.
edc.issuer.issuance.send.retry.base-delay.msxstring1000The base delay of retries in milliseconds. The n-th retry waits the base delay times 2 to the power of n, but at most the maximum delay.
edc.issuer.issuance.send.retry.max-delay.msxstring300000The maximum delay between two retries in milliseconds.
edc.issuer.issuance.send.connect-timeout.msxstring5000How long it may take to connect to the counterparty when a message is sent, in milliseconds.
edc.issuer.issuance.send.call-timeout.msxstring15000How long it may take to send a message and receive the response, in milliseconds. A message that times out is not retried right away, but by the state machine, like any other failure.
edc.iam.accesstoken.jti.validationxstringfalseActivates the JTI check: access tokens can only be used once to guard against replay attacks
edc.issuance.anonymous.allowedxstringfalseAllow anonymous onboarding
